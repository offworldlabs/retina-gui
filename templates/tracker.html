Reset the wedge?

This clears the 24 h max-hold wedge for this node. Tracks and detections are kept.

The wedge rebuilds as ADS-B-verified aircraft pass through, so expect it to be empty or partial for a while. Reset after moving or re-aiming the antenna. It also resets by itself when the receiver or transmitter position changes.
