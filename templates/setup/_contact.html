How can we reach you?

If something goes wrong with your node that we can see and you cannot, this is how we get in touch. It is only used to support you with your device.

Contact

The two-letter country the phone number belongs to, for example US or GB.

Every box is optional, and you can skip this step. Nothing here is published, none of it is checked, and it gives you no account or login. You can add or change it later under How We Reach You on the Configuration page.

Support

What our support team may do with this node when they are not at your location. Both are yours to change at any time under Remote Support on the Configuration page, and neither affects how the node works day to day.
