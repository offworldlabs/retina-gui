Auto-calibration

Your radio starts on generic settings that are not chosen for your site. This tries progressively more sensitive settings against your chosen tower and keeps the strongest one that does not overload the receiver.

The radar restarts at the end to pick up the new settings. You can run this again at any time from Configuration, and you should re-run it if you change a filter later.
